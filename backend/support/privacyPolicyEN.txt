========================================
KANMA CINEMAS - PRIVACY POLICY
========================================

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
- Personal Identifiers: Full name, email address, phone number, and optional date of birth.
- Booking & Financial Records: Transaction IDs, booked movies, seat preferences, and order history. Note: Full payment card details are handled directly by PCI-compliant payment gateways and are never stored on our servers.
- Technical & Device Data: IP address, operating system, browser specifications, and platform analytics cookies.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
- To generate tickets, confirm transactions, and deliver order confirmations via email/SMS.
- To notify you about changes to screening times or cancelled sessions.
- To improve our website performance, app features, and service quality.
- To send promotional discounts, newsletters, and loyalty rewards (only with your explicit opt-in consent).

3. DATA SHARING & THIRD PARTIES
- We do not sell, lease, or monetize your personal information to third parties.
- We share necessary minimal data with verified service providers (payment processors, cloud hosts, SMS gateways) solely to complete required business operations.
- Data may be disclosed if required by legal summons, court order, or official governmental directive.

4. DATA RETENTION & SECURITY
We implement industry-standard encryption protocols and administrative safeguards to keep your personal records secure against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

5. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS
You have the right to request a copy of your stored personal data, request corrections, or ask for your account and data to be permanently erased. Contact support@kanmacinemas.com for any privacy-related requests.
