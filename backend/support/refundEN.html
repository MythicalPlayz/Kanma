At Kanma Cinemas, your satisfaction is our priority.

We offer a flexible refund policy. You are eligible for a full refund if your booking meets all of the following criteria:

  • The request is made at least 30 minutes prior to showtime.
  • The physical ticket has not been printed (via box office staff or self-service kiosk).
  • The ticket was not redeemed through promotional discounts, bank offers, or reward points.
  • The ticket was not purchased as part of a package or bundle that is non-refundable.
  • The ticket was purchased online either through our website or mobile app.

Tickets purchased directly in person at the box office are final and non-refundable.

You can manage or cancel eligible bookings tied to your account here.

Kanma Cinemas reserves the right to decline refunds in the event of a Terms of Service violation or under exceptional circumstances at management's discretion.
